Cookie & Web Storage Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

Short version: We do not run ad-tech, retargeting, or behavioral profiling cookies on this site.

1. Browser storage

This site stores only your optional analytics choice in local storage. We do not use advertising, retargeting, or behavioral-profiling cookies.

2. Optional analytics

Analytics is off by default. If you accept it, Google Tag Manager loads Google Analytics 4 to measure page path, language, referral, and ordinary technical device/request information. We do not send contact-message contents or app portfolio data to analytics. Rejecting analytics does not limit the site.

3. Other external services

The current site loads presentation resources from Google Fonts, Tailwind CDN, and unpkg. These providers receive ordinary technical request data such as IP address, browser information, and time of request. Social, Google Play, and product links contact their providers only when you open them.

4. Your choice

Use the “Privacy settings” control on any page to accept, reject, or withdraw optional analytics. Your choice is stored locally in this browser and can also be cleared through browser settings.

Questions: info@smartdailyapps.com.
